WELCOME TO YOUR STUDIO

A few words to get you started.

  1. Make room for an idea.

    Type, paste, import a text file, or try a prompt. Add a title to keep things organized.

  2. Find the right voice.

    Choose a language and a device voice. Preview it, then adjust speed, pitch, and volume.

  3. Sit back and listen.

    Play, pause, resume, or stop. Reading view can highlight words when your voice supports word events.

  4. Keep what you love.

    Save up to 20 scripts with their voice settings, or export your words as a .txt file.

Browser support & privacy

Voices, pronunciation, pause behavior, and highlighting vary by device. Dictation needs microphone permission and may send audio to your browser provider. Some speech voices also use online services.

SpeechBot has no backend, account system, or analytics. Scripts are kept in this browser; clearing site data removes them. Private browsing and opening a different address may use separate storage.

Audio downloads are unavailable: browser speech synthesis provides playback, without an audio export file. Export .txt saves your script text.

Reading times and progress are estimates. Bangla playback requires a Bangla voice installed on your device. Dictation support varies by browser; use HTTPS or localhost.
